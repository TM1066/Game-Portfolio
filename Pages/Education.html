2021–2023: HSDC South Downs

OCR Level 2 IT Fundamentals - Distinction*

I returned to education through a Level 2 course covering the fundamentals of IT, having not attended secondary school. The course gave me a brief but valuable introduction to programming, which I took to immediately. Over the year, I developed several games, including Arcane Aristocracy and Thief, and helped classmates who found programming less enjoyable complete their own projects.

UAL Level 3 Games Design & Development - Distinction

After completing my Level 2 qualification with a Distinction*, I was keen to specialise in game development. Having already created several games, I was warmly welcomed onto the Games Design & Development course, where I developed my skills further. I also became more involved in college life by joining the Students' Union and starting a creative writing club! Although I completed fewer games during this course, I continued to develop my skills. I'm currently expanding my final project, "Polarity", which I plan to release on my itch.io page once it's finished.
