Bunny Hop
Action Key
Jump (Player 1) ↑
Move Left (Player 2) A / ←
Move Right (Player 2) D / →
Bunny Hop was the first game I developed after starting university and discovering the arcade machines in one of our buildings!

It was a great way to get back into making and sharing games. It's a simple game, but it turned out more polished than I expected and makes for a fun, quick break before lectures!

My girlfriend created the bunny sprites, giving me valuable experience in collaborating with an artist to bring a game to life.
