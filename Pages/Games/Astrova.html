The idea for Astrova came from a brainstorming session with fellow students at university. After finishing Bunny Hop and working on a few other projects, I was looking for a new idea suited to an arcade machine. I repurposed the ship sprite from a pixel art competition I had entered a few years earlier and built the rest of the game's visual style around it! The visuals are simple, but I'm proud of how the game feels to play. It remains one of the most popular games on the Arcademia arcade machines at the University of Lincoln!
